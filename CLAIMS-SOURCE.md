## Deliberately NOT carried over

These are the elements that make the current site read as untrustworthy. They were
removed rather than restyled — restyling them would have made an unsupported claim
look *more* credible, which is the opposite of the brief.

| Removed | Why |
|---|---|
| "ALLTRA COIN LUCKY DROP — Click HERE and Reward Yourself NOW!" | Prize/reward framing on a financial product is the single strongest scam signal on the page. |
| "To get it free all You need to do is get yourself some 11::11 Coin and Start your personal Gold backed Economy" | Free-money framing, and "free" is contradicted by the purchase it requires. |
| ASIC and AUSTRAC links in the footer | Linking regulators next to partner logos implies oversight or endorsement that the site never actually claims. |
| Empty "What Our Client Say" testimonial rail | A heading with nothing under it reads as abandoned. No testimonials were invented to fill it. |
| Identical feature lists across $220 → $6,500 tiers | Four prices for the same list invites the reader to assume the pricing is arbitrary. Tiers now differ only on what their own table says differs. |
| Production typos: "Valadator", "Regiser & Verify Email" | Spelling errors in a payment flow do more trust damage than any design choice. |
| Duplicated 7-step process carousel (rendered 3×), steps 01 and 02 near-identical | Deduplicated to five distinct steps. |
| "Copyright © 2023" | Three years stale on a site publishing 2026 posts. |
| Stock template assets still in place (`logo.png` is the vendor's "Qolle" mark) | Third-party mark, not Alltra's. |

## Facts the concept could NOT source — needs client input before this goes live

These belong on a trust-critical site and are absent from alltra.world entirely.
They are **not** in the concept, because inventing them is exactly what rule 8 forbids.

1. **ACN / ABN and registered office** for Ozz Metals Co. Pty Ltd.
2. **Named people** — no founder, director or team member is identified anywhere on the site.
3. **A phone number or postal address.** Contact is an email address and a form.
4. **Any independent audit, security review or reserve attestation** — critical for the
   1/1000-oz-of-gold claim in particular. Nothing of the kind is published.
5. **The actual legal status of HYBX.** Their own words are "functions similarly to a
   promissory note"; whether it is a financial product in Australia is not stated and
   is not a call MP Launch should make.
6. **Real logo artwork.** The only Alltra mark served is a 44×44 px artifact, unusable
   above that size. The concept uses a typographic lockup as a placeholder.
